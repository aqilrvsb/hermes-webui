# Meta Ads — Budget Rebalance
1. Pull ad-set performance (spend, ROAS/CPA, results) for the window.
2. Propose new budgets: shift toward top performers, cut/pause bottom ones; respect total budget and avoid >20-30% single-step swings (learning phase).
3. Show before/after table; on approval apply via `zernio` (Meta/FB ads tools: ads_*, ad_campaigns_*, ad_audiences_*).
