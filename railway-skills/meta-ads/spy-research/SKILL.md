# Spy & Research (2026)

Two jobs: (1) **Intel** — what's working in the market (Ad Library), (2) **Gold** — the customer's own words
(Reddit/reviews/surveys). Tiana's rule: **99% stop at Intel — that's the mistake.** Spying alone gives no edge
and you never learn *why* it worked. Use competitors for inspiration; never copy-paste.

## Meta Ad Library (facebook.com/ads/library) — free, login required
- **Set location = where ads are SHOWN** (use "All" / set to **Malaysia** for local), not where you/they are.
- **Sort/scan by Impressions, high→low (Heath).** Post-Andromeda, longevity alone is misleading (tons of ads
  sit active with zero spend). **Winner signal = high impressions + long run time.**
- **View INACTIVE ads** too (status toggle) — see what they stopped.
- **Workflow:** count a competitor's active ads (if they run 10–15× more, ramp your production) → inspect run
  dates, placements, primary text, all creative variations → **funnel-hack**: click the CTA, walk their
  landing page / VSL / lead magnet / WhatsApp flow.
- **Cluster** repeated headlines/angles/offers = what's actually working. Map where their ads send traffic.
- **Validate demand:** search the PROBLEM not the product ("balas WhatsApp lambat", "auto reply customer").
  3–5 brands advertising a similar solution = a validated market.

## Who to model
- Model **smaller competitors you can replicate**; skip giants (big-influencer/pro-production ads you can't copy).
- **Cross-niche (Nick): recreate winners from OTHER niches** in yours — strongest, least-cloned source. He
  refuses to even look at direct competitors. Tools: Trend Track ("Shops" w/ Meta-pixel + traffic filters)
  surfaces scaling shops → their top/longest-running ads + transcripts.
- Big brands' ads often fail (they don't need them to work — CT). Study people who NEED their ads to convert.

## The DIG method (Tiana) — D-ata, I-ntel, G-old
- **Data:** your own winners/losers, recurring benefits/pains/objections from past ads + CRM.
- **Intel:** competitor Ad Library (above).
- **Gold (the differentiator): the prospect's own language.**
  - **Reddit** — `site:reddit.com [problem]`; also r/malaysia, r/MalaysianPF, local FB groups, Lowyat. Prioritise
    threads with high comment counts + recent dates. Search problem terms for problem-aware, solution terms
    ("is X worth it / just hype") for solution-aware. "99% of winning ads come from Reddit" (Tiana).
  - **Reviews:** Amazon/Shopee/Lazada **1-star + 5-star**, and **3-star** (Andy: reveals product gaps = your
    angle). Competitor negative reviews → what to position against (Nick, Jordan).
  - **Customer surveys** (Typeform to existing customers — not post-purchase).
  - **YouTube comments** sorted by most-liked = highest resonance.
- **AI extraction prompt:** paste reviews/threads → *"Extract pain points, benefits they desperately want, and
  objections — be specific, include the real language they use."* Still read threads yourself ("brain sponge").

## Output of this skill
A research pack the strategist/copywriter consumes: **personas, awareness/sophistication per persona, ranked
angles (with the customer's exact words), competitor offers/hooks to beat, and validated demand.** For
PeningBot/PeningLab: mine MY SME owners' real WhatsApp-overwhelm language; check Malaysian Ad Library for
WhatsApp-automation / chatbot / "auto reply" competitors and their CTWA hooks.
