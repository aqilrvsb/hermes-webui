# skills/COPYWRITING_MY.md — Malaysian ad copy (Bahasa Pasar) for PeningBot/PeningLab

Product promise: **auto-reply WhatsApp 24/7 so the seller never loses a lead.**
Audience: MY SME owners / online sellers (fashion, F&B, services, dropship, agents).

## Tone & language
- **~70% casual BM + 30% English** ("Manglish"/bahasa pasar). BM carries emotion/pain; keep English on business/tech nouns locals never translate: *customer, order, reply, auto-reply, follow-up, lead, sales, online, trial, link, chat.*
- NOT formal "baku" (sounds like a govt notice) and NOT pure English (feels like an MNC, not local).
- Slang to use **sparingly (1–3 per ad)**: jom, bos, korang, confirm, auto, penat/pening, rugi, terbaik/power/mantap, settle/gerak/on; particles lah/je/kan/dah for warmth.
- **Lean on the brand name:** "pening" = headache → "Bisnes pening sebab WA tak terjawab? PeningBot uruskan."
- Emoji: intentional, ~1 per benefit line, one 👇 above CTA. Not a wall (looks scammy). Common: ✅🔥👇📲💬⏰😩💰📈🎉.

## Real MY pain points (use as angles, verbatim-ish)
1. Customer tanya tengah malam → esok baru perasan → dah beli kat orang lain.
2. Reply lambat = customer lari ke seller lain yang reply dulu (speed = sale; MY expects reply < 1h).
3. 100 mesej sama tiap hari: "harga?" "COD?" "ada stock?"
4. Lead hilang celah-celah WA / IG DM / Messenger.
5. Penat, tak sempat makan sebab asyik balas chat (burnout/time).
6. Tutup kedai = takde sales (no 24/7).

## Frameworks (PAS is the default for cold MY traffic)
- **PAS** (Problem→Agitate→Solve): *"Customer WhatsApp pukul 1 pagi… esok baru nampak, dia dah beli kat seller lain — rugi lagi. PeningBot balas auto dalam saat, lead tak lari lagi."*
- **AIDA:** *"WA korang meletup tapi tak sempat balas? 😩 PeningBot auto-reply 24/7… confirm tak rugi lead lagi. Jom cuba free 👇"*
- **BAB:** *"Before: 100 chat 'harga?' tiap hari. After: semua auto-reply, korang fokus close sales. Bridge: PeningBot buat semua tu — setup 5 minit."*
- **Hormozi offer stack:** *"Auto-reply 24/7 + auto follow-up + jawab harga/stock automatik + setup percuma — RM0 untuk 7 hari. Tak puas hati? Cancel, takde bayar. Jom try."*

## Hook lines (line 1 = everything; pick one per ad)
- Penat balas WhatsApp satu-satu sampai lewat malam? 😩
- Customer tanya, korang tak sempat reply… dia beli kat orang lain. 😤
- Bos, berapa banyak sales korang dah rugi sebab reply lambat?
- Sambil korang tidur, bisnes korang patut tetap jalan. 😴➡️💰
- WhatsApp meletup masa Raya, korang sorang je yang layan? 🎉📲
- Reply lambat 5 minit je, customer dah lari. ⏰
- Macam mana seller lain reply customer pukul 3 pagi? Diorang guna ni. 👀
- Berhenti jadi "admin WhatsApp" untuk bisnes sendiri.
- Tutup kedai, sales pun berhenti? Tak payah dah.

## CTAs (pair one with every ad; route to WhatsApp/CTWA)
- Jom cuba FREE 7 hari — takde kad kredit.
- Klik "Send Message" / WhatsApp kami sekarang 📲
- PM "PENING" dekat WhatsApp, kami tunjuk macam mana.
- Slot free trial terhad — gerak sekarang. ⏰
- Stop rugi customer mulai hari ni.

## Offers that work in MY
Free 7-day trial (no card, explicit cancel) · "Auto-reply 24/7" as the headline · time-saved ("jimat 3 jam sehari") · loss-aversion ("jangan rugi lead") · done-for-you setup ("kami setup, 5 minit on") · festive promo (Raya/CNY/Deepavali/payday/11.11) · social proof ("1,000+ seller MY guna").

## Don'ts
Formal baku · slang overload · scam-y "untung besar tanpa usaha" (Meta reject + distrust) · religious/racial/political claims · robotic tone with no human-escalation mention · CTA with no WhatsApp/trial path. Keep modest/halal-safe visuals.
