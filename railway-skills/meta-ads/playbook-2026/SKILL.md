# Meta Ads Playbook 2026 (the marketing brain)

Synthesized from Ben Heath (26 videos) + Antonio Ventre (15 videos), 2026. Use it for
all strategy, creative, copy, testing and scaling. Product = **PeningBot/PeningLab**
(WhatsApp-automation SaaS, Malaysia). Always resolve the live ad account before acting.

## 1. Andromeda changed the game
Meta's **Andromeda** AI runs targeting/optimization now → **creative is the #1 lever**, and
it **collapses near-identical ads into ONE entity** (only one gets spend). So:
- Every ad must be a **DIFFERENT entity** = different visual concept + unique copy. Aim **15–20 diverse ads** per ad set.
- Meta optimizes the winning **SEQUENCE**, not single ads → never turn off a high-spend / low-ROAS top-funnel ad; it feeds the winners.

## 2. Structure — CONSOLIDATE
- **One CBO campaign + one ad set per product/pixel**, many diverse ads inside. Pool conversions (one ad set @ 50+/wk learns; many small ones don't).
- **Stop separating cold vs warm / TOFU-MOFU-BOFU** — custom audiences are now *suggestions*, so a "cold" set spends on warm anyway. Put founder/demo/testimonial ads in one ad set; Meta auto-sequences.
- Split into separate campaigns only by product *range* (PeningBot vs PeningLab). Never duplicate a campaign (auction overlap).

## 3. Judge by INCREMENTALITY, not reported ROAS
- A high front-end ROAS can be fake (existing-customer purchases). Use Breakdown → audience segment (judge **new-customer** results) + the **incremental attribution** column. Learn on 7-day-click (more data); **kill/scale on incremental**.
- Frequency discipline: scale only winners with high ROAS **and** low frequency (<~1.7); kill high-frequency/low-ROAS.
- Funnel mix ≈ **50% top / 30% mid / 20% bottom** awareness; statics skew mid/bottom (ceiling ~RM low) — add low-frequency top-funnel (problem-aware) to lower bottom-funnel frequency and unlock scale.

## 4. Targeting
Broad + **Value Rules** (bid more for the most valuable segment). Detailed interests = suggestions only. Only **location** is a hard constraint. Don't over-narrow.

## 5. Testing — big things first
**Offer → Angle → Style → Hook → (last) copy/colour.** Offer is #1 (most "no sales" = offer problem). One angle per ad. Use Meta's Creative Testing Tool (ad level; set metric to cost-per-lead/sale) so new creative actually spends. Expect most tests to fail; one winner pays for many (VC mindset). Quality > volume.

## 6. Creative
- **Hook = first ~3s** = biggest lever (track hook-rate = 3s-views ÷ impressions; good 20–33%). **Recombine** a proven body + a proven hook.
- Net-new concepts (research Pinterest/Reddit; make the VISUAL match the angle/pain) beat copied/AI-recycled ads. Use AI to iterate winners.
- Partnership/UGC with real faces = cheat code.
- **Images** (gpt-image-2 = best text/layout; nano-banana = best realism/UGC) for the diverse batch; **1–3 videos** (Veo/Gemini, 9:16, **~16–20 words dialogue per 8s clip**, `(no subtitles)`, name a light source + texture for realism). Sizes: 1:1 feed, 9:16 stories/reels; keep text in the safe zone.

## 7. Malaysian copy (bahasa pasar) — see also COPYWRITING_MY style
~70% casual BM + 30% English business nouns (customer/reply/auto/lead/sales). Lean on "pening/penat balas WA". Frameworks: **PAS** (default cold), AIDA, BAB, Hormozi offer-stack. Pain angles: after-hours lost sales, reply-lambat = customer lari, 100x "harga?" daily, lead hilang celah WA/IG, burnout, tutup-kedai-no-sales. Hooks: "Penat balas WhatsApp sampai lewat malam? 😩" / "Customer tanya tengah malam, esok dah beli kat orang lain." CTAs: "Jom cuba FREE 7 hari", "PM 'PENING'". Offers: free trial (no card), auto-reply 24/7, time-saved, festive (Raya/CNY/payday/11.11). Modest/halal-safe; no scam-y "untung besar"; keep a human-escalation note.

## 8. Scaling
Stepped budget (bigger % at low spend, smaller % high; wait 5–7 days; find the ROAS ceiling) OR automated +3%/day when cost/result < target (daily budgets; window last 3/7/14 days). **Bid cap = AOV ÷ target ROAS** (for leads: lead-value ÷ target) on a separate campaign of proven winners — spends more on high-demand days.

## 9. Funnel → sales
Match ad angle to a congruent landing page by awareness (unaware→advertorial/quiz, problem-aware→listicle, product-aware→PDP). For PeningBot/PeningLab use **click-to-WhatsApp (CTWA)** funnels (cheap, MY-preferred). Diagnose leaks: clicks-no-leads = landing/offer; leads-no-sales = offer/closing; sales-unprofitable = value-optimize / upsell. Optimize **value of conversions**, not count.

## 10. Account safety
Complete business verification. If disabled: request review → Meta AI Business Assistant → escalate to a human agent → your Meta rep. Avoid bought accounts / "recovery services".
