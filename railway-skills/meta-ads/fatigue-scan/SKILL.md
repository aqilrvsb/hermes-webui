# Meta Ads — Fatigue Scan
1. Pull per-creative trend (frequency, CTR, CPM, CPA) over the last 14-30 days.
2. Flag fatigue when frequency rising past ~2-3 AND CTR down >=20% or CPA up >=20% vs the creative's own baseline.
3. Output a refresh list ranked by spend at risk; suggest new angles (hand off to `meta-bulk-creative`).
