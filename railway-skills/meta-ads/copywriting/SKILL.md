# Copywriting — angles, hooks, scripts (2026) + Malaysian copy

Messaging drives the creative (Tiana: "copy-first"). Pick **persona → awareness → sophistication → angle →
hook**, in that order, tied to the week's account goal. The visual is language too — match it to the persona.

## 1. Awareness stages (Eugene Schwartz) — pick before writing
- **Unaware** → don't know they have the problem. Hardest; usually AVOID (Tiana). Only for taboo, commodity,
  or already-scaled brands. Unaware hook may NOT name problem/solution/product/price (Nick) — open with a
  **Big Win, Symptom, Question, or Statistic**.
- **Problem-aware = "the money shot"** (desperate, motivated) → lead with the PROBLEM.
- **Solution-aware** → show the solution/mechanism in action; comparison vs alternatives.
- **Product-aware** → product up front, social proof, offer.
- **Most-aware** → simple: offer + scarcity ("20% off, only X left").
- **Biggest acquisition opportunity = the middle 3** (Jordan). For PeningBot/PeningLab, MY SME owners are
  mostly **problem-aware** (drowning in WhatsApp) → lead with the pain.

## 2. Sophistication (how tired the market is) — escalate the claim
1 Beginner: big bold simple claim · 2 Explorer: bigger/differentiated claim · 3 Researcher: expose the
**mechanism** (how it works) · 4 Skeptic: enlarge mechanism + **heavy proof**, lead with credibility ·
5 Hopeless: drop claims, address **identity & self-blame** ("it's not your fault"). (Tiana)

## 3. The hook (first 3s = the whole game)
- Its only job: stop the scroll. Track **hook rate ≥30%**. Recombine a proven body + a proven hook.
- **8 trigger words (Tiana):** Finally, Challenge, Simple, Avoid, Warning, New, Proven (back it w/ a number),
  Only (scarcity). Use near the CTA for TOF; lead with it in the hook for BOF.
- Cold audiences → benefit/problem hook; warm → objection-led openers OK.

## 4. Offer = 3 P's (Nick) + Value Equation (Hormozi)
- **Person** (one avatar/offer) · **Perceived value** · **Packaging.** "50% off" is an *offer enhancer*, not
  the offer. Perceived value = (Dream outcome × perceived likelihood) ÷ (time delay × effort).
- **Value Bridge / ICP doc:** current situation → product (the bridge) → desired situation, each split into
  **physical** (what they literally experience) and **mental** (fear, insecurity, hopelessness from failed
  solutions). Keep 2–3 scaling ICPs per product in a sheet, grow it over time.

## 5. Script frameworks (sequence is critical — cold won't watch "here's our product" first)
- **PAS** (Problem-Agitate-Solve) = default cold. AIDA, BAB, "3 Reasons Why", Problem-Solution also work.
- **Transformation (Jordan):** Hook (before/after proof) → curiosity loop → product intro → agitate problem →
  education/discovery → after-state → stack benefits → **unique mechanism** → benefits tied to deep desire → CTA.
- **Unaware masterclass (Nick):** Big-Win/Symptom/Question/Stat hook → problem → problem-with-current-solution
  → new mechanism → build perceived value (show the R&D) → unexpected twist (2nd hook) → handle objection live
  → David-vs-Goliath proof → desired state → offer enhancer → guarantee.
- **Always link feature → function → deep emotional desire** ("not all-day energy → his presence/attention").
- **Yapper/UGC scripts:** still scripted — write the "ums/likes/pauses" in; "talk like to a friend."

## 6. The 6 levels of proof (escalate vs skepticism — 92% of people distrust ads)
L1 Tell me (hyper-specific stats: "1,472 studies", not "thousands") → L2 Show receipts (screenshots/reviews on
screen) → L3 Have them tell me (UGC/expert/founder) → L4 Show me (before/after, comparison) → L5 Demonstration
(product working live) → L6 Extreme demonstration. **Use odd, precise numbers** ("boosts 197.5%", "53,207
women") — they read as real data. (Tiana)

## 7. Customer's own words (the un-AI edge)
Best copy = the prospect's exact language. Mine **Reddit, review sites, YouTube comments (sort by most-liked),
customer surveys** (see `spy-research`). "Enter the conversation already in their head."

## 8. Malaysian copy (bahasa pasar) — for PeningBot / PeningLab
- **~70% casual BM + 30% English business nouns** (customer, reply, auto, lead, sales, follow-up). Manglish OK.
- **Core pain:** "pening/penat balas WhatsApp", after-hours lost sales, reply lambat = customer lari, 100×
  "harga?" sehari, lead hilang celah WA/IG, burnout, tutup kedai → takde sales.
- **Hooks:** "Penat balas WhatsApp sampai lewat malam? 😩" / "Customer tanya tengah malam, esok dah beli kat
  orang lain." / "Owner kedai 40-an, sorang balas 100 WhatsApp sehari?"
- **CTAs (website-SALES — drive to the site, NOT WhatsApp):** "Mula sekarang di peningbot.com", "Cuba plan RM35", "Subscribe Pro di peninglab.com", "Daftar sekarang". Never "PM us".
- **Offers:** neither product has a free trial — lead with the low entry tier + refund/risk-reversal (see `offer-design`); festive promos (Raya/CNY/Merdeka/payday/11.11).
- **Tone:** modest, halal-safe; NO scam-y "untung besar/cepat kaya".
- **Local proof:** specific numbers, real testimonial screenshots, founder/owner ads for trust.
- **Brand-specific (never mix):** PeningBot pains = lost sales/time (WhatsApp overwhelm, admin cost); PeningLab pains = expensive/slow content, competitors out-posting. Pull the right brand's pains/hooks from `_products/<brand>.md`.

## 9. Ad ↔ landing-page congruence (critical — see `landing-funnel`, `website-sales-funnel`)
The landing-page **headline MUST continue the ad's hook/angle** (problem-aware ad → page leads with that
problem). Congruence ad→page is a top conversion lever (Jordan: 1.6%→3.6%). Write the ad copy, the page
headline, and the CTA as a matched set; CTA wording stays continuous ad→page→checkout.
