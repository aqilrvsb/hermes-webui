# PRODUCT BRIEF — PeningBot (www.peningbot.com)

The single source of truth for all PeningBot creative, copy, offers, and targeting. Pixel
`986352420917190` (+ CAPI, deduped by payment_id). Objective = **OUTCOME_SALES → website →
paid monthly subscription** (CHIP). The Product Analyst refreshes this weekly from the site.

## What it is
Malaysian **B2B SaaS** for SME owners (usahawan). Two independent products on one platform:
- **A — WhatsApp AI Chatbot:** connect any WhatsApp number → AI auto-replies 24/7 in natural BM
  using the owner's prompt → captures customer details → notifies owner on a closing. 5-minute
  setup (scan QR + paste prompt). Unofficial (QR) or Official (WhatsApp Business API, verified).
- **B — Hourly Ads Reporting:** connect FB/TikTok/Google Ads → hourly report pushed to WhatsApp;
  reply with commands (`==stop F1 1`, `==up F1 1 +10`) to control campaigns without opening Ads Manager.

## Who it's for (persona)
MY SME owner, **25–45**, BM-first, sells online/services (Shopee/Lazada/own site/DM, or clinic/salon/
coaching), ticket RM50–2000, **5–100 WhatsApp msgs/day**, already runs ads RM500–10k/mo, solo or 1–2
staff, margin-tight. Secondary: agency owner running ads for 3–20 clients. **NOT** enterprise, English-
only, or regulated (banking/insurance/medical).

## Pains (lead with these)
- Customer messages at 11pm, owner asleep → 23 unread by morning, 14 buy from competitor (~RM200/lost sale).
- Manual replies during family time → tension at home.
- Checking Ads Manager 10×/day → ~2h/day wasted.
- Hiring an admin = RM2,500–4,000/mo + EPF.
- Spending RM5k/mo on ads but not knowing which campaign is profitable.
- Sales-page framing: *"Setiap jam customer tunggu reply, anda hilang RM200. 73% usahawan Malaysia rugi
  closing sebab lambat reply WhatsApp lebih 2 jam."*

## Offer & pricing (the purchase = this)
- **Chatbot:** Starter **RM35** (1 device) · Pro **RM65** (2) · Premium **RM95** (3) /month.
- **Ads Reporting add-on:** **RM12/slot/30 days** (1 slot = 1 ad account, up to 10).
- CHIP (FPX/TNG/Boost/GrabPay/card). Cancel anytime, **7-day refund** (technical issues).
- **NO free trial** (each bot needs the owner's OpenAI credit + setup). Don't imply a free tier.
- Customer also pays OpenRouter credits (~RM10–50/mo, 0% markup) — be upfront.

## USPs (top 5)
1. **5-minit setup** (genuinely true). 2. **Native Bahasa Melayu** (not robot-translated). 3. **Hourly ads
reporting via WhatsApp** (unique — no other tool does this). 4. **Independent billing** (chatbot OR ads,
not forced bundle). 5. **Bring-your-own-AI** (any OpenRouter model, 0% markup).

## Brand voice & visuals
"anda" (formal-casual); "bro"/"akak" OK in informal ad copy. Malay+English code-switch (keep
**setup, auto-reply, ROAS, closing** English). Specific numbers always (RM4,000, 5 minit, 23 unread).
**Pain first, then solve** — never lead with a feature. No corporate-speak. Visual: **WhatsApp green
#25D366** + purple #7c3aed, warm/friendly. Never lie ("5 minit" must be true; no "300% ROAS").

## Website-sales funnel (objective = SALES)
Ad (pain hook) → **www.peningbot.com** (pain mockup → cost calculator → transformation → testimonials) →
"Mula Sekarang" → CHIP checkout → **Purchase** (paid subscription) fires Pixel + CAPI. CTA = "Mula
sekarang di peningbot.com" / "Cuba plan RM35" — NOT "PM us". At RM4/day, optimize a higher-funnel event
(InitiateCheckout / begin-checkout) first, switch to Purchase as volume grows.

## AVOID

promising "never ban"; non-MY targeting; implying a free trial.
