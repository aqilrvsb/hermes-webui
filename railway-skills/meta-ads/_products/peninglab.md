## What it is
Malaysian **AI marketing studio for UGC video + image generation**, built for **TikTok Shop affiliates &
small sellers**. Paste a TikTok Shop / Shopee MY link → AI scrapes the product → **generates 10 UGC-style
videos in ~3 minutes** (configurable avatar persona, BM voice/script). Also: Original Video (Veo 3.1 8s,
Sora 2, Gemini Omni 10s, Seedance), Image Studio (nano-banana-pro, gpt-image-2), Clone Video, Story Telling,
an **MCP API** (peninglab-mcp), and a Chrome extension that scrapes TikTok Shop/Shopee from the user's session.

## Who it's for (persona)
MY **TikTok Shop affiliate / e-com seller**, **22–40**, earns RM1–15k/mo, sells fashion/kitchen/skincare/
gadgets/dropship, posts 1–5 TikToks/wk wants 3/day, **can't afford RM300–500/video** UGC, tried CapCut+VO and
finds it slow. Secondary: agency (5–20 client accounts, wants MCP automation). Tertiary: hijabi/camera-shy
creator (wants avatar). **NOT** enterprise, English-only, regulated, or live-streamers.
